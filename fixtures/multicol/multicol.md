<dl class="two" id="glossary">
<dt>Bleed</dt><dd>The band beyond the page edge into which ink meant to reach the edge is printed, so that an imperfect cut still finds ink.</dd>
<dt>Blank page</dt><dd>A page with no content, made by a forced break that names a side. It still has a number and a parity.</dd>
<dt>Break</dt><dd>The place where one page ends and the next begins. Forced by the author, or chosen by the engine.</dd>
<dt>Counter</dt><dd>A number CSS keeps for you, reset and incremented by elements and read by generated content.</dd>
<dt>Crop marks</dt><dd>Short lines in the corners of a sheet that show the cutter where the page is.</dd>
<dt>Composed mode</dt><dd>Cicero&rsquo;s second way of working, in which it sets the lines itself instead of reading the browser&rsquo;s.</dd>
<dt>Cut</dt><dd>A break seen from the box it divides: the place in the tree where one fragment stops and the next resumes.</dd>
<dt>First page</dt><dd>The page <code>:first</code> selects: the first page of the document, whatever its name.</dd>
<dt>Flow</dt><dd>The content of a page area, poured from the top; a float or a column is a flow beside it.</dd>
<dt>Folio</dt><dd>The page number, and by extension the margin box that shows it.</dd>
<dt>Fragment</dt><dd>The piece of a box that falls on one page, when a page edge cuts the box.</dd>
<dt>Keep</dt><dd>An instruction not to break: inside a box, or between a heading and its text.</dd>
<dt>Margin box</dt><dd>One of the sixteen boxes in a page&rsquo;s margin, filled by <code>content</code> from the stylesheet.</dd>
<dt>Leader</dt><dd>A row of dots that joins a title to its page number. Specified as <code>leader()</code>, drawn by neither engine.</dd>
<dt>Measure</dt><dd>The width of a line of text: the page area, less whatever a float or a column takes from it.</dd>
<dt>Named page</dt><dd>A page type with a name of its own, asked for by the <code>page</code> property.</dd>
<dt>Orphan</dt><dd>A line of a paragraph left alone at the foot of a page.</dd>
<dt>Page area</dt><dd>The content area of the page box: what the margins leave, and where the text goes.</dd>
<dt>Page box</dt><dd>The box the size of the sheet, described by <code>@page</code>.</dd>
<dt>Parity</dt><dd>Whether a page is a left or a right one, worked out by the engine as it counts.</dd>
<dt>Remainder</dt><dd>What is left of a box after a page edge has cut it, carried to the next page.</dd>
<dt>Recto</dt><dd>A right-hand page, the front of a leaf.</dd>
<dt>Running head</dt><dd>A line repeated at the head of the pages, copied from the text by a named string or a running element.</dd>
<dt>Spread</dt><dd>Two facing pages, a verso and a recto, seen together as the reader sees them.</dd>
<dt>Verso</dt><dd>A left-hand page, the back of a leaf.</dd>
<dt>Widow</dt><dd>A line of a paragraph carried alone to the head of the next page.</dd>
</dl>
